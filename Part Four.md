## Part Four

Technical Analysis is based on using market prices to determine the value of a company stock. Technical analysis includes using time graphs to assess the value of the firm's stock over time and to make predictions. Moreover, technical analysis investing assumes that the market corrects itself and the market price of the stock is the “true value” of the stock. 

The benefits of technical analysis include the easy availability of historical information. This information can be used in time series software (e.g., Minitab) to make predictions and graphs of the stock price over time. Moreover, the produced graphs are easy to read and comprehend (simply plotting the price of the stock across time). 

The disadvantages of technical analysis are that it does not account for important information like the degree of the company’s innovation or the company’s investing (or lack thereof) in research and development. 

Technical analysis will be useful in the term project because historical information is easily available and the same can be used to make predictions and assessments. Moreover, the conclusions can be used in conjunction with other information (e.g., fundamental analysis information) to make decisions and recommendations 
In Conclusion, technical analysis can be used in conjunction with fundamental analysis and any other kind of available information, to make evidence based decisions and recommendations. These recommendations can be used by the corporation to make sustainable, profitable, and well-reasoned operational changes.
