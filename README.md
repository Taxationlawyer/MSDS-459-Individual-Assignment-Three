# MSDS-459-Individual-Assignment-Three
*This is the Individual Assignment Three for the MSDS 459 Course at Northwestern University.*

The magic of artificial intelligence (AI) is taking the world by storm. AI can make superhuman decisions and predictions about financial investments. All you need to do is press a button and you get magic. And you will become rich from that magic. All of the previous words are complete sarcasm—save for the hype of AI.

People are constantly looking for the holy grail of financial algorithms to get rich. Although AI is not magic, AI (along with traditional machine learning methods) can be used to make sound and well-reasoned decisions, evidence-based judgements. 

In short, the five parts in this device are a good example of how a “totality of the circumstances” approach can be used to make evidence-based decisions that are supported by sound judgment. The “totality of the circumstances" approach includes AI, traditional machine learning methods--also referred to as statistical learning methods (e.g., classification systems), information extraction, fundamental investment analysis, technical investment analysis, and sentiment investment analysis. These factors (along with any other piece of available information) can be used to make sound judgments and evidence-based decisions.
